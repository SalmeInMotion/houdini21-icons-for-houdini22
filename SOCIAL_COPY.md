# Social media copy

## Short post

Miss the Houdini 21 icons in Houdini 22? We built a free, reversible Windows mod that restores the classic icon artwork—including the main viewport toolbars—while preserving icons that are genuinely new in H22.

It does not modify Houdini or redistribute SideFX assets. The overlay is generated locally from your own H21 and H22 installations and can be removed cleanly at any time.

## Detailed post

Houdini 22 introduces a redesigned icon set, but many artists prefer the clarity and familiarity of the Houdini 21 icons. We created an open-source, reversible Windows mod that brings the classic artwork back to Houdini 22.

The installer compares the icon archives from your local Houdini 21 and Houdini 22 installations, restores changed icons that have a matching counterpart, and keeps every icon that exists only in Houdini 22. It also rebuilds H22's small-icon cache, so the classic artwork appears in the viewport and toolbar controls where the redesign is most noticeable.

Nothing inside the Houdini installation is modified, no SideFX artwork is included in the repository, and uninstalling returns Houdini 22 to its original appearance.
